How it works

Shorter routes are more likely to become parents. Crossover combines their routes; mutation reverses a random section. The best route survives every generation.

Point and population changes apply with New map or Restart evolution. Mutation and speed update live. Evolution searches for good routes; it does not guarantee the optimal route.
